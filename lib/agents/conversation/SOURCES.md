# Conversation agent: sources

`knowledge.ts` is distilled in our own words from public guidance (web research, 2026-10-11). No text is copied. Mostly practitioner guides and popular psychology, not controlled studies, so treat it as best practice, not proof. Thin spots: little hard evidence on chatty/opinionated styles and on backchannel wording.

| Area in knowledge.ts | Drawn from |
|---|---|
| Small talk topics (FORD), comment before question, don't interrogate, move on if hesitant | Harvard FAS Career Services FORD post; Leadership Freak; Hyperbound; Fortune (2025) |
| Follow-ups show responsiveness and raise liking | Huang et al. 2017 "It doesn't hurt to ask" (via APS summary, CNBC) |
| Open vs closed questions, follow up on what the speaker raised, reflecting | Pressbooks mentoring chapter; Univ. of Kentucky extension handout; UConn Rudd Center handout |
| Listener feedback timing and why it matters | Bavelas et al. listener-response work; Boudin et al. 2024; TU Graz 2025 backchannel study |
| Shift vs support responses, conversational narcissism, balance | Charles Derber, *The Pursuit of Attention* (via Art of Manliness and Next Big Idea Club summaries) |
| Silences: not always a problem, don't rush to fill, open question or setting comment restarts | succeedsocially.com; The Brief Lab |
| Shy partner: patience, shared interests, open questions, low pressure | succeedsocially.com; assorted tips articles (low quality, used only for common themes) |
| Opinionated partner: curiosity first, acknowledge and add | Psychology Today; Toastmasters |
| Topic transitions: acknowledge then bridge, environment hook, wait for thought to finish | Talaera; Time/AOL "change the subject"; podcast summary |
