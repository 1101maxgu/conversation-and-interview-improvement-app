# Sales (buyer) agent: sources

`knowledge.ts` is distilled in our own words from public guidance (web research, 2026-10-11). No text is copied. Almost all sources are practitioner and sales-vendor blogs (written from the seller's side, often marketing), not controlled studies, so treat the behaviour as best practice, not proof. We invert seller advice into buyer behaviour.

| Area in knowledge.ts | Drawn from |
|---|---|
| Objection types (need, trust, price, timing, authority, status quo, implementation); objections are signals, some are real mismatches | 11x common sales objections; Highspot objection handling; Woodpecker; Mindtickle; Zoomforth; Supered |
| Feature dumping, jargon, generic claims, benefits only count once the buyer confirms them | Sandler (features/benefits); SBI Growth; Selling Power; The Sales Hunter |
| Negotiation: anchoring, conditional concessions, trading terms for price, ultimatums, competitive leverage | Racklify supplier negotiation tactics; Contracko negotiation glossary |
| Stalls ("think about it", "check with my team") hide an open concern or a real decision-maker | Sandler Acknowledge-Explore-Resolve; Lightico; Floor Trends handling stalls; Salesforce objection types |
| Decision process, economic buyer, champion, paper process (MEDDIC seen from the buyer's side) | SalesGear MEDDIC; DealHub glossary; Salesforce MEDDIC intro; Prospeo MEDDIC vs MEDDPICC |
| Cold call: reflex brush-off, short honest opener, bounded ask, gatekeepers | Tenbound/Graph8; Sellfire; Apollo gatekeeper guide; Prospeo |
| Discovery questions: open, outcome-based, calm tone, tailored to persona and stage | Gong; Highspot; Sifthub; A79; Weflow |
| Closing: pressure and fake deadlines erode trust; direct ask plus a mutual action plan | CustomerThink high-pressure closing; Sandler old-school closes; HubSpot closing guide; mutual-action-plan skill write-up |

Gaps: little hard data on buyer personas (busy exec, skeptic, price-focused, friendly-undecided are our own simplified archetypes), on typical concession sizes, and on skeptic-specific trust-building.
