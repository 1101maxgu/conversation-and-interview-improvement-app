# Case agent sources

All knowledge is distilled into our own words. Sources are mostly practitioner material (coaching sites and forum answers from former consultants and interviewers), not official firm publications. Practice varies by firm, office and interviewer, so treat the behaviour as typical, not guaranteed.

| Area | Used for | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
